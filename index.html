About FIRST

FIRST (For Inspiration and Recognition of Science and Technology) is a global nonprofit organization that designs accessible, innovative programs that motivate young people to pursue opportunities in science, technology, engineering, and mathematics (STEM).

Through FIRST programs, students engage in exciting challenges that build problem-solving and collaboration skills. Our FRC (FIRST Robotics Competition) team designs and builds robots to compete against other teams in intense, high-energy matches. Every season brings new challenges, new learning opportunities, and the chance to showcase innovation and teamwork on a regional and national scale.

FIRST isn't just about building robots. It's about building the next generation of leaders, engineers, and innovators who will shape our future.

NEW FOR 2026-2027: FTC Team Launch

Exciting news! We're expanding our FIRST family by launching an FTC (FIRST Tech Challenge) team for the 2026-2027 season. This is a fantastic opportunity for middle school and younger high school students to get involved in competitive robotics!

What is FTC? FIRST Tech Challenge gives teams of students the opportunity to build and program robots to compete in head-to-head matches. It's smaller and more accessible than FRC, making it perfect for beginners and younger students.

Why Join Our FTC Team?

  • Learn robotics, programming, and engineering fundamentals
  • Join a supportive, experienced team community
  • Compete at district and state championships
  • Develop leadership and teamwork skills
  • Be part of a tradition of innovation and excellence

History

Since 2017, we have been working hard to build robots and compete in competitions. We qualified for the World Championship our rookie year, and more recently last year in 2025. We plan on attending again this year for our fourth time. Over the years, our team has consistently grown in both size and skill, developing strong capabilities in design, programming, and manufacturing. Competing in the FIRST in Michigan district, we regularly participate in district events and the Michigan State Championship, earning multiple awards along the way, including honors such as the Team Sustainability Award, Judges' Award, and Autonomous Award. Our continued success is driven not only by our performance on the field, but also by our commitment to outreach, teamwork, and mentoring the next generation of students. As the Perry RAMBOTS, we strive to improve each season, pushing our limits while representing our school and community with pride.
