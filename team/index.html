About the Team

We are a team of 21 wonderful students and 2 wise mentors to help lead the students and help students realize their full potential. Each student has a cruical role in making the robot work, from 3D modeling, using the CNC machine to manufacture parts, or wiring the robot. We also have students who do more business related work, such as managing funds, making schedules, making scouting forms, and building up a following on social media.
