About Guppy

Guppy is our 2025 robot designed for the Reefscape game. It features a swerve drive system, an intake system, an elevator, and a shooter. Guppy performed well in 2 district matches where it competed in the playoffs of both events. At the Michigan State Chamionship Guppy won the Autonomous Award for the Consumers Energy Division and led the Rambots as the Captain of Alliance 4. Guppy competed bravely at the World Championship in Houston as well!
